The 5 Core Dye Tube & Cone Profiles

Every tube and cone is manufactured to rigorous dimensional tolerances with round-edge perforations and micro-textured finishes.

Compressible

D-SPRING

Compressible Dye Tube

Axially compressible lattice structure accommodates high yarn shrinkage under boiling dye liquor. Eliminates density gradients.

  • ✓ Uniform Axial Compression
  • ✓ Prevents Yarn Entrapment
  • ✓ Maximum Liquor Circulation
Rigid

D-STIFF

Rigid Dye Tube

High-rigidity engineered barrel resistant to excessive centrifugal pressure and aggressive yarn collapse forces in dense packages.

  • ✓ Heavy-Duty Non-Compressible
  • ✓ High-Tension Package Winding
  • ✓ Extended Autoclave Lifespan
Stackable

D-STACK

Stackable Dye Tube

Self-centering interlocking ends allow seamless column stacking on dye vessel spindles without separate intermediate separator plates.

  • ✓ Interlocking Self-Centering
  • ✓ Saves Dye Spindle Loading Time
  • ✓ Eliminates Intermediate Spacers
Perforated

D-CONE

Perforated Dye Cone

Precision taper conical dye package with balanced radial perforations for uniform inside-to-outside flow and trouble-free unwinding.

  • ✓ Optimized Staggered Holes
  • ✓ High-Flow Conical Geometry
  • ✓ Snag-Free Package Delivery
Sale Yarn

S-CONE

Sale Yarn Cone

Solid engineered cone with micro-embossed velvet grip surface designed for high-speed automatic winding and smooth yarn take-off.

  • ✓ Micro-Grooved Grip Surface
  • ✓ Zero Yarn Slough-Off
  • ✓ High-Speed Auto-Coner Ready

HYDRAULIC FLOW DYNAMICS

Designed for Complete Dye Uniformity

In high-pressure package dyeing machines, uneven liquor circulation causes shade variations from the inside core to the outside circumference. Autotex tubes solve this at the root.

01

Optimized Staggered Apertures

Our proprietary matrix perforation delivers uniform hydraulic cross-flow pressure across every winding layer, reducing cycle time and chemical consumption.

02

Smooth Radiused Hole Edges

Every orifice is precision mold-radiused. No micro-burrs, no sharp plastic edges. Zero fiber snagging during high-speed take-off at 1,200+ m/min.

03

Thermal & Chemical Endurance

Molded with prime virgin polypropylene and nylon blends with proprietary thermal stabilizers, resisting disperse dye carriers, acid baths, and repeated 135°C cycles.
